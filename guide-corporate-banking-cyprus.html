Corporate Banking in Cyprus: Practical Setup Guide

By Geonique Corporate Team · Updated March 2026

Opening a Cyprus corporate account is usually straightforward when the company file is complete and the business model is explained clearly. Most delays come from incomplete KYC packs, unclear transaction narratives, or missing proof of operational substance.

Before you apply

  • Prepare certified incorporation documents, UBO identification, and proof of address.
  • Draft a clear activity profile: what you sell, where clients are located, expected annual turnover, and average transaction size.
  • Prepare counterpart information for key suppliers and customers.

Choosing local bank vs EMI

Local banks may fit businesses needing broader lending relationships and traditional branch support. EMIs can provide faster onboarding and efficient multi-currency operations. Many international companies use both: EMI for daily operations and bank account for reserve and relationship depth.

KYC interview preparation

Decision teams want consistency. Your shareholder profile, website narrative, invoices, and interview answers should match. If your website says "software consulting," avoid describing activity as "general trading" in onboarding forms.

First 90 days after opening

  • Keep transaction behavior close to projected volumes.
  • Maintain invoice/contract evidence for incoming and outgoing transfers.
  • Respond quickly to compliance follow-up requests.
  • Document any major changes in geography or business model in advance.
