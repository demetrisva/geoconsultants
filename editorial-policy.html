Editorial Policy

Effective date: 6 March 2026 · Owner: Geonique Consultants, Nicosia, Cyprus.

1) Purpose

Our website content is designed to help founders, investors, and operations teams understand Cyprus legal and corporate procedures in plain language. We publish practical guidance, not AI-generated filler or scraped text.

2) Authorship and review

  • Drafts are written by members of our legal, tax, and corporate services teams.
  • Each article is reviewed for legal accuracy, clarity, and client usefulness before publication.
  • Where rules change, content is updated and the review date is refreshed.

3) Sources and reliability

We base our content on applicable Cyprus legislation, Registrar practice, tax authority guidance, and direct procedural experience from client engagements. We avoid unverifiable claims and exaggerated marketing statements.

4) Corrections and updates

If a reader identifies an error, we investigate promptly. Material inaccuracies are corrected and clearly updated on-page. To report an issue, email info@geoconsultants.eu.

5) Commercial transparency

Our articles may discuss services we provide. This commercial relationship is disclosed by default on this page. We do not accept paid placement for legal or tax opinions.

6) Legal disclaimer

Website content is general information and does not constitute legal, tax, or financial advice for your specific situation. Always seek tailored advice before taking action.
