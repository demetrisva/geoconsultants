Operating in Cyprus: Substance, Tax and Governance Basics

By Geonique Advisory Desk · Updated March 2026

Many companies incorporate correctly but lose efficiency later due to weak operating routines. The key is to treat compliance as part of operations, not a year-end event.

Governance routines

  • Hold regular board meetings and retain decisions in clear minutes.
  • Keep a register of directors, shareholders, and key resolutions updated.
  • Align contractual authority with actual decision-makers.

Tax and accounting discipline

  • Issue invoices promptly with correct VAT treatment.
  • Reconcile bank and EMI accounts monthly.
  • Maintain supporting documentation for cross-border transactions.

Substance checkpoints

Where beneficial tax treatment or treaty access is expected, maintain evidence of real management and operational presence in Cyprus. Typical evidence includes local decision-making, local service providers, and proper record keeping.

When to ask for advice

Seek support before entering new markets, changing ownership, onboarding related-party contracts, or adding new payment corridors. Early review usually prevents expensive remediation later.
